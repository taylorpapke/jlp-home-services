Who We Are

JLP Home Services is a locally owned and operated home improvement company proudly serving Nashville, Clarksville, and Franklin, Tennessee. Rural Renovations is our Ohio division.

We believe your home should be a place of comfort, beauty, and functionality, and we bring integrity, craftsmanship, and genuine care to every project.

Our mission is simple: to deliver high-quality workmanship with honest communication and dependable service. Whether it’s a small repair or a major renovation, we approach each job with the same attention to detail and dedication to excellence.

What sets us apart is our commitment to personalized service. We take the time to listen to your needs, offer clear guidance, and ensure the final result matches your vision.
